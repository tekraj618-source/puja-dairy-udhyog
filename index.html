About Puja Dairy Udhyog

Puja Dairy Udhyog is a trusted local dairy business serving Dhangadhi and the surrounding community for more than five years. We provide fresh, quality dairy products with a caring focus on hygiene, taste and customer satisfaction.

From the morning glass of milk to the dahi shared at family meals, we are proud to be part of everyday life in our community.
